Privacy policy

Updated: 12 November 2026

Who we are

Kuvello runs a WhatsApp bot that books appointments and sends reminders for businesses ("the business"). Privacy contact: support@kuvello.com.

Two kinds of users

Customers of a business, who write to the business on WhatsApp; and the business itself, which connects its WhatsApp number to Kuvello.

What we keep about a business's customers

  • The WhatsApp number the message came from.
  • The messages sent to the bot and the bot's replies.
  • The name given for an appointment, and the appointment (service, date, time, and with whom).
  • A request to stop receiving messages ("stop"), and consent to reminders when recorded.
  • The bot never asks for or keeps ID numbers, payment details or medical information, and does not answer medical questions.

What we keep about a business

  • The business's name, opening hours, services, prices, staff and their hours, and the wording the business approved.
  • The business's WhatsApp Business account and phone number identifiers, and the access token Meta issues when the business connects (Embedded Signup). The token is kept in a protected file on our server, never shown to anyone and never sent anywhere except to Meta.
  • The business staff's dashboard logins, and a log of every change made there.

Why

Booking, moving and cancelling appointments; reminders; answering common questions; handing a conversation to the business's staff; stopping messages for anyone who asks.

Who sees it

  • The business you contacted, and Kuvello staff for operating the service only.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) carries the messages, under WhatsApp's privacy policy.
  • The server is at DigitalOcean, in a data centre in Amsterdam (EU).
  • When the business connects a Google calendar, the appointment is written to the business's calendar with the name given.
  • A message the bot's rules cannot read may be sent to OpenAI for classification only; the reply to the customer always comes from the fixed sentences.
  • Data is not sold and not shared with anyone else.

How long

  • Conversation transcripts are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Appointment details are kept as long as the appointment, its reminders and the business's diary need them.
  • Database backups are kept for two days, then deleted.
  • When a business disconnects, its access token is deleted and its data is deleted on request.

Deletion and requests

  • To stop receiving messages: send "stop" to the bot.
  • To ask for all your data to be deleted: write to support@kuvello.com from your number or with your appointment details. We act within 30 days. You can also ask the business.
  • More deletion instructions: https://bot.kuvello.com/data-deletion

Changes

Any change to this policy is published here with its date.
