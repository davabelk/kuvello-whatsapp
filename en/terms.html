Terms of service

Updated: 12 November 2026

The service

Kuvello provides businesses with a WhatsApp bot for appointments, reminders and common questions, on the business's own WhatsApp number.

The business's account

  • The business connects its WhatsApp Business account through Meta's official flow, and remains the owner of the account and the number.
  • The business is responsible for the wording it approves and the hours, prices and services it sets.
  • The business pays Meta directly for messages, at Meta's rates.

WhatsApp's rules

The business and Kuvello follow the WhatsApp Business Policy and WhatsApp's Commerce Policy. Messages the business starts are sent only as Meta-approved templates, and never to anyone who asked to stop.

What the bot does not do

  • The bot gives no medical, legal or professional advice and makes no diagnosis. Such questions go to the business's staff.
  • The bot never confirms an appointment that was not actually written to the diary.

Availability

We make every effort to keep the service running, but it also depends on Meta's and the hosting provider's services. During a fault the customer is given the business's phone.

Ending

A business can disconnect at any time. On disconnecting, its access token is deleted, and its data is deleted on request (see the privacy policy).
