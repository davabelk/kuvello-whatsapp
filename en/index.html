What the bot does

Booking in a few taps

The customer picks a day and a time from a list inside WhatsApp, types a name — and it is in the diary.

Moving and cancelling

Customers move or cancel on their own, with a confirmation before any cancellation.

Reminders

A reminder before the appointment with buttons: I will be there, change time, cancel.

Fixed answers

Opening hours, prices, address and what to bring — in sentences the business approved.

Hand-over to staff

Anyone who asks for a person, or writes something the bot does not understand, gets the phone number or leaves a message for the team.

Stops when asked

A customer who writes "stop" gets no more messages.

How a business connects

Connecting takes about 10 minutes, from a phone. We send you a personal link, and then:

  1. Open the link and tap "Start". A Meta window opens.
  2. Log in with the Facebook account that runs the business.
  3. Choose the number you already use in the WhatsApp Business app. The app and your chats stay.
  4. Confirm Meta's message in the app and type the code.
  5. Check the business name and allow the two permissions Meta shows.
  6. Add a payment card in WhatsApp Manager — Meta bills the business directly for messages. It can wait.

From the first minute the bot answers customers who wrote to you. Reminders and other messages the business starts switch on by themselves, each as soon as Meta approves its template (usually within 24 hours).

Questions

Do I need a new number?

No. You connect the number you have, and the app keeps working. You can still answer yourself from the app.

What if a customer writes something urgent?

The bot never diagnoses or improvises. Its behaviour is written in advance: offer a time, and if none fits, the business's phone or a message for the team. For clinics, outside opening hours, it also gives the emergency number.

Who writes the replies?

The sentences are fixed and approved in advance. A language model never writes messages to customers.

How long does setup take?

Connecting takes about 10 minutes. Hours, services and prices are set up together, and then the bot answers.

Who pays Meta?

The business, directly to Meta, for messages the business sends (such as reminders). Kuvello takes no commission on messages.

What about privacy?

Only what an appointment needs: the WhatsApp number, a name, the appointment and the conversation. Conversation transcripts are deleted after 30 days. No ID numbers or payment details are asked for. Full details in the privacy policy.

Which language?

The bot speaks Hebrew.
