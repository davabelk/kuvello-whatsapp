Services

What Kuvello does for a dental clinic

Practical WhatsApp automation, set up to follow WhatsApp's messaging and opt-in rules, and run so a clinic's front desk gets consistent, automated patient conversations without adding staff.

  • WhatsApp Business API chatbot setup

    Provisioning and configuration of the clinic's WhatsApp Business API number, approved message templates, and an automated bot that greets patients, answers common questions, and routes each conversation to the right next step.

  • Appointment booking & reminder automation

    Booking, rescheduling, and cancellation flows for dental clinics, with confirmation messages and timed reminders that help reduce no-shows — all handled inside the patient's own WhatsApp chat.

  • Patient FAQ & intake automation

    Instant answers to the questions patients ask most — hours, location, pricing, insurance, and how to prepare — plus a structured intake that collects the details a clinic needs before the visit.

  • CRM integration

    Connection to CRM platforms such as GoHighLevel / LeadConnector, so patient contacts, appointments, and conversation history stay in sync with the tools the clinic already uses.

About

About Kuvello

Kuvello is operated by david belikov as an individual sole operator based in Ramle, Israel. It is a hands-on service, not a company — there is no separate registered legal entity, no staff, and no outside investors.

The current project is a WhatsApp appointment-booking and patient-communication assistant built for a dental clinic. It runs on the clinic's WhatsApp number and takes care of the routine back-and-forth that would otherwise tie up the front desk.

Here is how it works in practice: a patient messages the clinic's WhatsApp number, and the bot answers frequently asked questions, collects the details needed to book, and books or reschedules the appointment. It sends reminders before the visit, and when a request needs a person — an unusual question, a complaint, or anything outside its scope — it hands the conversation off to a member of the clinic's team.

How it works

From first message to confirmed booking

A simple flow that runs on the clinic's own WhatsApp number.

  1. The patient sends a message

    A patient messages the clinic's WhatsApp number to ask a question or request an appointment.

  2. The bot replies and collects details

    Kuvello answers common questions instantly and gathers the information needed to book — name, preferred day, and reason for the visit.

  3. It books and reminds

    The bot books, reschedules, or cancels the appointment and sends timed reminders before the visit to help reduce no-shows.

  4. A person steps in when needed

    Anything the bot cannot handle is handed off to a member of the clinic's team, with the conversation history intact.
