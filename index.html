How it works

From first message to confirmed booking

A simple flow that runs on the clinic's own WhatsApp number.

  1. The patient sends a message

    A patient messages the clinic's WhatsApp number to ask a question or request an appointment.

  2. The bot replies and collects details

    Kuvello answers common questions instantly and gathers the information needed to book — name, preferred day, and reason for the visit.

  3. It books and reminds

    The bot books, reschedules, or cancels the appointment and sends timed reminders before the visit to help reduce no-shows.

  4. A person steps in when needed

    Anything the bot cannot handle is handed off to a member of the clinic's team, with the conversation history intact.

Questions clinics ask

Straight answers

Do patients need to install anything?

No. Patients use the WhatsApp they already have — they just message the clinic's number the same way they'd message anyone else.

Does it work in Hebrew?

Yes. I build the conversations in Hebrew, English, or both, and the bot replies in the language the patient writes in.

What does it cost?

A one-time setup fee plus a flat monthly fee, agreed before we start — no surprises later. There's no long-term contract: it runs month to month, and you can stop anytime.

How long does setup take?

Usually one to two weeks. Most of that is WhatsApp's own review of the clinic's number and message templates — building the bot itself takes me days, not months.

What happens when the bot gets stuck?

It stops guessing and hands the conversation to your front desk with the chat history attached. Patients are never left arguing with a machine.

Is this allowed by WhatsApp?

Yes — it runs on the official WhatsApp Business Platform, messages only people who opted in, and every patient can reply STOP at any time. WhatsApp is a trademark of Meta Platforms, Inc.; Kuvello is independent and not affiliated with Meta.
