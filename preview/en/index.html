Emergency protocol

In an emergency, the bot doesn't improvise

When a patient writes about pain, swelling or bleeding, no language model decides the reply. The behaviour is written in code, and the sentences the patient receives are fixed and approved in advance.

Detected by fixed rules

Words like pain, swelling, bleeding or a broken tooth move the conversation onto the emergency path. The rules run before the language model and override it.

The nearest slot, or the clinic's phone

The bot offers the nearest available times. Any reply that isn't a clear choice, such as "maybe" or "I don't know", stops the conversation and gives the clinic's phone in the same message. It never asks again and never ends on a question.

Outside opening hours

The very first reply, next to the times, already points to Magen David Adom 101 or the nearest emergency room.

No medical answers

The bot answers no medical questions and never judges severity; it refers the patient to staff. When a patient describes a life-threatening non-dental situation, such as chest pain or trouble breathing, the 101 line is added too.

Staff see it at once

An urgent request goes to the top of the staff attention queue, flagged as an emergency, and stays there until someone marks it handled.

No "you're booked" without a verified write

The bot confirms an appointment only after the diary has confirmed the booking. If it wasn't verified, the patient gets the clinic's phone, not a confirmation.

Patient data

What's stored, where, and for how long

Where
On a dedicated Kuvello server in an Amsterdam data centre (DigitalOcean, EU). The messages themselves travel through Meta's WhatsApp.
What
Name, WhatsApp number, appointment details and the conversation with the bot. The bot never asks for an ID number: a block in the code stops any such request from being sent.
How long
Conversation transcripts are deleted automatically after 30 days. Server backups are deleted after three days at most. Appointment records are kept until deletion is requested.
Deletion
A patient who sends "הסר" (stop) gets no further messages, immediately. A request to delete all data is completed within 30 days. Data deletion instructions

FAQ

What clinics ask

Do patients need to install anything?

No. They message the clinic's WhatsApp number, as they always have.

Which language does the bot speak?

Hebrew. A message in another language gets a reply that the service is available in Hebrew only.

What happens when the bot doesn't understand?

It asks once for the message in other words. If it's still unclear, it gives the clinic's phone. Staff see the conversation and can continue it themselves.

Is this allowed by WhatsApp?

Yes. Kuvello runs on Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform, and a patient who writes "הסר" (stop) gets no further messages.

Who will I be working with?

Directly with the person who built the system. No call centre, no middlemen.
